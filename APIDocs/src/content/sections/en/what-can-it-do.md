ITM Platform's MCP server connects your project management data to the AI assistant of your choice. MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets AI tools talk to external services. Once connected, your AI assistant can read your portfolio, query budgets, and even create tasks or log risks -- all through natural conversation.

It works with Claude, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Codex, Windsurf, JetBrains, and any other client that supports MCP. You choose the AI; the MCP server handles the bridge to ITM Platform.

### Example conversations

> **You:** "Which projects are behind schedule?"
>
> The AI searches your active projects, compares planned vs. actual dates, and lists the ones that are slipping.

> **You:** "Create a high-priority task in Project Alpha due next Friday"
>
> The AI creates the task in ITM Platform and confirms the details.

> **You:** "Give me a budget summary for the Digital Transformation program"
>
> The AI pulls revenue, cost, and margin data and presents a clear breakdown.

> **You:** "What risks are open across my portfolio?"
>
> The AI aggregates risks from all your projects and highlights the highest-impact ones.
